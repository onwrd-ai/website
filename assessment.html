How it runs

Two weeks. A handful of conversations.

The total ask of your team is one short survey and a few interviews. We do the rest, and you see the findings live.

  1. Day 0

    Pre-survey, about 20 minutes

    Tools, workflows, goals, and what you've already tried. It lets the interviews go deep instead of covering basics.

  2. Week 1

    Interviews, recorded

    45–60 minutes with you and any department heads, then 30–45 minutes each with two to five of the people closest to the repetitive work. Nothing is attributed to anyone by name. Owners see the business from above; the gold is in what the team does at 2pm on a Tuesday.

  3. Week 2

    Analysis

    Every workflow scored for frequency, time cost, structure and automation suitability. Findings mapped across seven dimensions and plotted on an impact-versus-effort matrix. Every AI recommendation has to pass the four-filter test or it doesn't make the report.

  4. Delivery

    A live 90-minute debrief

    A working session through the report with your leadership, not a PDF lobbed over the wall. Report, recording and roadmap are yours within 24 hours.

The baseline

Most businesses land at Level 1 or 2. That's the starting point, not a grade.

We score on behaviour, not ambition. A business that "uses AI a lot" but can't name the tools and the outcomes is Level 2 at best, and that's fine. The roadmap is how you move.

  • L0UnawareNo usage, no awareness
  • L1CuriousInformal individual use
  • L2ExperimentingA few tools, no integration
  • L3AdoptingCoordinated, owned, measured
  • L4ScalingAcross functions, real ROI
  • L5AI nativeEmbedded in how you operate
