Privacy

What this site collects, and what we do with it.

Last updated 16 November 2026. Plain language on purpose. Onwrd. is operated by Matt Walker, a sole proprietor in Victoria, British Columbia, Canada.

The contact form

If you use the form, we receive your name, email address, and whatever you write about your business. It is sent to us by Formspree, a form service, and lands in our email. We use it only to reply to you. We don't add you to a mailing list, and we don't sell or share it.

Booking a call

The booking link opens Calendly, which asks for your name and email to create the appointment. Calendly's own privacy policy applies to that step.

Analytics and cookies

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts; both receive the standard technical information any web request carries, such as your IP address. If we add a privacy-respecting analytics tool later, this page will say so.

Client work

Information gathered during an assessment or retainer (surveys, interview recordings, transcripts, documents) is covered by the engagement agreement, not this page. In short: recordings are for note-taking only, nothing is attributed to a team member by name, we use business-tier AI tools that don't train on your data, and we handle personal information under Canada's PIPEDA and BC's Personal Information Protection Act.
