Four parts

Everything the retainer includes.

01

An intake form, before the first call

It inventories your manual processes across sales, operations, finance and marketing, the tools you already have, and what you've tried before. Then the question that sets the agenda: which process would you pay most to never do again?

02

Two 45-minute working sessions a month

Your screen, your keyboard. We build alongside you, never for you. The first session covers the foundations: workspace, connectors, context. Every session after that takes on one real process.

Voice or text, answered the same business day. It's where most of the small questions get solved, and it stops you stalling between sessions.

04

Every session logged: recording, summary, action items both ways, and a running inventory of everything built. When you want to know whether the retainer is earning its keep, it's all in one place.

A month, roughly

Audit. Optimise. Automate. In that order, never skipped.

Jumping straight to automation is the most common failure we see. Automating a broken process just makes the inefficiency faster.

  1. Session one

    Audit the process

    You perform it manually, end to end, while we watch and ask questions. Every step, every hand-off, every copy-and-paste gets mapped. Most owners are surprised by what their own process contains.

  2. Between sessions

    Optimise it

    We cut the fat together over async messages. A 15-step process is usually a 9-step process, and only the steps that survive get automated.

  3. Session two

    Automate it, with you

    Plain automation where the work is deterministic, the smallest AI model that clears the bar where reasoning or language is needed, people where judgement matters. Built in your tools, documented in the hub, owned by you.

  4. Next month

    The next process

    The assessment's roadmap sets the order. Each quarter we re-check every model and tool against lighter options, because the cheapest choice changes monthly.

Terms, plainly

A hard cap on clients, stated honestly.

    Then month to month with 30 days' notice, billed in advance. The price is quoted on the call and doesn't change mid-term.

  • 02

    Capacity is limited on purpose

    This is hands-on work, so we take a fixed number of retainer clients and don't exceed it. That's why the assessment comes first: it tells us both whether the work is worth doing.

  • 03

    You own everything

    Built in your workspace, documented in your hub, maintainable without us. Software licences and API usage are yours, billed at cost. No lock-in by design.

  • 04

    Governance comes early

    A one-page AI use policy, a shadow-AI inventory and data-handling rules for every connected tool, all delivered in the first weeks. Almost no small business has them.
